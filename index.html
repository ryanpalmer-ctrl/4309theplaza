Why builders move on this

Skip the line at the city

The slow part of an infill townhome project is getting it designed and through the city. On this site that work started in 2023 and finished in February 2026.

A builder buys the result: approved civil plans, an approved residential plan set, NCDOT driveway approval and a recorded plat that already splits the land into four lots. Each finished home sells on its own lot, and the design work is done, so you can go straight to permits and pricing.

Already done

  • Sealed civil set: site, grading, erosion control, utilities, landscape and tree save, traffic control (Gateway Design Group)
  • Stamped architectural set for all four units (Urban Design Group, v5)
  • Recorded plat: Lots 1-4 + Green Area, Map Book 75, Page 668
  • Tree-save green area designed in: 2,378 sf, 16.1% of the site (15% required)
